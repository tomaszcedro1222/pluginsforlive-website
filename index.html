Available Plugins

These plugins were designed for real live productions made in vMix. Tested and used during hundreds of hours of live production and streaming.

Auto Camera

The plugin automatically switches cameras based on the person currently speaking. It is ideal for podcasts, debates, interviews and multi-camera live productions. The trial is free for 2 months and includes full functionality, so you can decide after real production use whether a subscription is worthwhile.

  • Automatic camera switching
  • Active microphone detection
  • Supports multiple cameras and microphones
  • PTZ camera support with automated shot changes
  • Optional AI voice recognition
  • Camera Readiness for field-production workflows
  • Reduces manual switching
  • Ideal for podcasts and livestreams

Ducking Audio

Ducking Audio automatically lowers selected audio sources when another source becomes active. It is designed for live productions, podcasts, conferences and simultaneous interpretation workflows where one signal should temporarily reduce the level of another.

  • Automatic audio ducking in vMix
  • Works with selected inputs and audio channels
  • Useful for interpretation, voice-over and live events
  • Adjustable thresholds, hold time and fade times
  • Designed for real live production workflows

Free Online Tools

Simple browser-based apps. You can use them during live productions without installing additional software.

Questions app

A tool for collecting and displaying questions during live productions. Questions can be added from a phone, tablet or computer and then displayed as a Browser Source in vMix.

  • Add questions in real time
  • Works on phones and computers
  • Full-screen mode for live production
  • Ideal for webinars and podcasts
Open App

Rocket Timer

A simple countdown timer designed for live productions. It runs directly in the browser and can be added to vMix as a Browser Input.

  • Countdown during live production
  • Full-screen mode
  • Works as a Browser Input in vMix
  • Useful for events and livestreams
Open App

Rocket Prompter

A simple teleprompter for reading text during live streams, webinars, presentations and video recordings. It runs directly in the browser and allows smooth text scrolling.

  • Automatic text scrolling
  • Reading speed control
  • Full-screen mode
  • Ideal for webinars and video recordings
