vMix microphone automation

Automatic microphone mixing for vMix

AutoMix is designed to manage microphone levels automatically, making conversations clearer and the live audio mix more stable.

The idea

A steadier conversation mix with less manual correction

AutoMix works in a way similar to professional audio automixers. It manages microphone levels to reduce chaos in conversations and help maintain a more consistent live mix.

Automatic level control

Manage microphone levels without making every correction manually.

Clearer speech

Keep conversations easier to follow when several microphones are part of the production.

Simple workflow

The product is being designed around straightforward configuration for live use.

Intended uses

  • Video and audio podcasts
  • Panel discussions and debates
  • Interviews with multiple microphones
  • Webinars and conferences
  • Live talk formats produced in vMix

Current availability

AutoMix is not yet available for download or purchase. This page will be updated when the product becomes available.
