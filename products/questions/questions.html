Select a color, then tap the dash next to a question

Questions

Work-in-progress app for displaying questions to hosts during live productions.

Developed and published by AZ Tomasz Cedro under the Plugins For Live brand.

Working version: 1.6 mobile hard fix

Other plugins are available at pluginsforlive.com.

The website includes free plugins as well as selected paid plugins.

Press Enter in the bottom field to add a question. Tap the dash to apply the selected color. Delete mode: tap Delete, then tap the question you want to remove.
